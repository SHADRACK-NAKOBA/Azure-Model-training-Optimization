# Step 8 – Share your learnings on LinkedIn and in the community

[⬅ Previous: Optimize with hyperparameter tuning](07-hyperparameter-tuning.md) | [Next: Delete resources ➡](09-cleanup.md)

A strong professional profile is essential in today's job market. Sharing your labs and projects on LinkedIn shows employers and connections that you have hands-on experience and keep building your skills. If you don't have a LinkedIn profile yet, create one now.

## 8.1 On LinkedIn

1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com and click **Start a post**.
2. Click the **image** icon and add your screenshots: the completed command job's metrics, and the sweep job's **Trials** tab.
3. Write the post in your own words, with the steps you took. For example:
   - *Provisioned an Azure ML workspace and compute with the Azure CLI*
   - *Converted a notebook into a parameterised, function-based training script*
   - *Ran it as a tracked command job with MLflow metrics*
   - *Tuned the regularization rate with a sweep job running trials in parallel on a compute cluster*
   - Your result: for example, *"best Accuracy 0.774 with reg_rate = 0.01"*
   - Hashtags: `#Azure #AzureMachineLearning #MLOps #MachineLearning #MLflow #AI`
4. **Tag Prince** in the post (type `@` and the name, then pick it from the list) so it can be shared further to increase your reach: **Prince – [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/)**
5. Click **Post**.

> Don't post screenshots that show your subscription ID, keys or email address.

## 8.2 Share the win in the community

Post your screenshots in the Prince community under your **Progress Diary** (create one if you haven't yet). It builds your confidence and inspires others to do the hands-on labs.
